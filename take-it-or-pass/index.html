How the math works
  • Total miles = loaded + deadhead.
  • Trip cost = your cost per mile × total miles + extra costs.
  • Profit = rate − trip cost.
  • Take it when profit per total mile meets your target. Counter when you'd make money but less than your target. Pass when the load loses money.
  • Counter at least = (cost per mile + target) × total miles + extras, rounded up to the next $25.
  • Your numbers stay on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
